# Code
Register of the operations in the robot memory for the competition: Open rounds and obstacles challenges. Every code is made on lego mindstorm, right below yo will find information of the operations, that are on the archives on the folders above

# Open round: Movement and directional 
The robot has a total of 30 programs, we choose one depending on the situation, the first 9 are for moving clockwise, depending on what quadrant the round starts, the next 9 are the same but counterclockwise, then we have 6 programs in case the round doesn't start on a quadrant, but starts on the center of them, there we have 6 centers, 6 clockwise and 6 counterwise.
